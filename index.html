My Story

have always been a builder of small worlds. I start with a question, a craving or a hunch and keep going until it becomes something other people can hold, open and come back to.

What began as curiosity grew into a studio. Under amaris ct (amaris creative tech), I design and ship my own sites and apps. Each is a room in a larger house, made with equal care for how it looks and how it feels to use.

My work moves easily between the serious and the delightful: independent research on human consciousness and machine intelligence on one side, and on the other an app that finds the closest place serving the dessert you can’t stop thinking about.

Right now I’m tending all of it at once: writing, designing, launching a local business and learning in public. Not one grand project, but a garden that keeps blooming.

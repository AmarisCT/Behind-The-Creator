Dear friend, what this garden holds

Her Story

Jasleen has always been a builder of small worlds. She starts with a question, a craving or a hunch and keeps going until it becomes something other people can hold, open and come back to.

The Studio

Under amaris ct (amaris creative tech), she designs and ships her own sites and apps. Each one is a room in a larger house, made with equal care for how it looks and how it feels to use.

The Spirit

Her work moves between the serious and the delightful: research into consciousness and machine intelligence on one wall, an app that finds the dessert you’re craving on the next, and a parrot that reads tarot down the hall.

the collection

At present the collection holds 6 works, each one hung beside a masterpiece from the centuries before it.

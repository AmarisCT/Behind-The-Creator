Her Story

Jasleen has always been a builder of small worlds: the kind of person who starts with a question, a craving or a hunch and keeps going until it becomes something other people can hold, open and come back to.

What began as curiosity grew into a studio. Under amaris ct (amaris creative tech), she designs and ships her own sites and apps, each one its own room in a larger house, each built with the same care for how it looks and how it feels to use.

Her work moves easily between the serious and the delightful: independent research on human consciousness and machine intelligence on one side, and on the other an app that finds the closest place serving the dessert you can’t stop thinking about.

Now she is tending all of it at once: writing, designing, launching a local business and learning in public. Not one grand project, but a garden that keeps blooming.
